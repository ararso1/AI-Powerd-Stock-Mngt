## 1. Executive summary

Leli Trading operates a multi-stage coffee business: cherry intake, parchment and green processing, warehouse storage, roasting and grinding for the local market, and export fulfilment. Today, much of that chain still depends on paper, spreadsheets, and verbal coordination across warehouses. For ownership, the central pain is not “having software” — it is **losing the thread of coffee as it moves from one warehouse and process step to the next**.

When every kilogram cannot be tied to a lot, a location, a process run, and a responsible user, three things erode quietly:

1. **Physical stock** — undocumented moisture loss, shrinkage, rejects, and unrecorded transfers.  
2. **Financial visibility** — purchase cost, process yield, local margin, and export receivables that cannot be reconciled to the same bag of coffee.  
3. **Management control** — delayed reports, disputed balances between stores, and decisions made on yesterday’s numbers.

Csolve Technology proposes to implement **Csolve**, a purpose-built Coffee Stock and Warehouse Management System that digitizes and integrates Leli Trading’s full supply chain — from cherry receipt through local sale and export shipment — with **lot-level traceability**, **multi-warehouse inventory**, **role-based approvals**, and **executive dashboards**.

**Investment:** ETB **660,000** (excluding VAT), payable in three milestones aligned to delivery phases (40% / 35% / 25%).  

**Outcome for Leli Trading:** one operational truth across warehouses; fewer silent losses; faster close of day and month; and a platform that keeps the business competitive as Ethiopian coffee trade continues to digitize.

> **Value proposition:** Csolve turns coffee movement from a warehouse headache into a controlled, auditable, real-time operation — protecting stock, cash, and Leli Trading’s market position over the next three to five years.

## 2. Understanding Leli Trading’s operations

Leli Trading’s value chain is inherently complex. Coffee changes **form**, **location**, and **commercial channel** many times before it becomes revenue:

```
Supplier / farmer
    → Cherry receiving & weighing
    → Parchment processing, drying & storage
    → Hulling, cleaning, grading → Green coffee warehouses
    → Transfers between locations
         ├→ Roasting / grinding / packaging → Local sales
         └→ Export allocation → staging → shipment → buyer payment
```

Each arrow is a point where kilograms, quality grade, and accountability can diverge if records are not tied together. Manual processes make that divergence almost inevitable once volume and warehouse count grow.

Csolve is designed around this exact chain. Coffee is never “anonymous warehouse quantity”: every coffee stock movement references a **lot**, a **location**, and a **traceable event** (collection, process, transfer, sale, export ship, adjustment). That design matches how Leli Trading already thinks about coffee — and replaces fragmented notebooks with a single system of record.

## 3. Current challenges and the cost of staying manual

### 3.1 The main business bottleneck

**Tracking every step across different warehouses is the primary operational bottleneck for ownership.**

Typical day-to-day symptoms include:

| Challenge | What ownership experiences |
|-----------|----------------------------|
| Multi-warehouse blind spots | Cherry at intake, parchment in drying, green in store A, roast stock in store B — balances that do not reconcile without phone calls |
| Process yield opacity | Cleaning, hulling, roasting, and packaging losses that are known on the floor but not captured consistently |
| Split commercial channels | Local roast sales and export allocations competing for the same green stock without hard reservation |
| Weak accountability | Adjustments and transfers without clear approval or user trail |
| Late management information | Stock, purchase, sales, and export figures assembled after the fact — too late for intervention |
| Document and audit pressure | Export dossiers, lot history, and financial trails that take days to reconstruct |

The bottleneck is not a single warehouse; it is **the absence of a continuous digital thread** between warehouses and process stages.

### 3.2 What Leli Trading is losing — year by year

The following figures are **indicative impact scenarios** for a mid-scale Ethiopian coffee trading operation of Leli Trading’s profile. They are not a substitute for Leli Trading’s own audited accounts; they illustrate the annual cost of remaining largely manual, using conservative industry-aligned assumptions commonly observed in coffee trading and processing (shrinkage, moisture, undocumented movement, and working-capital drag).

**Assumed illustrative annual coffee throughput for modelling:** ~1,200 metric tonnes green-equivalent handled across intake, process, warehouse, local, and export channels.

| Loss / drag category | Conservative annual estimate | Basis (illustrative) |
|----------------------|------------------------------|----------------------|
| Unrecorded process & moisture shrinkage | ETB **1.8M – 3.6M** | 1.5%–3% of handled value escaping formal loss codes |
| Inventory variance & “missing” bags between stores | ETB **900K – 2.1M** | 0.8%–1.8% variance from weak transfer / count controls |
| Reject & quality write-offs without full trace | ETB **400K – 900K** | Reject coffee not held as controlled stock with reason codes |
| Working-capital drag (slow stock / receivable clarity) | ETB **600K – 1.2M** | Extra financing / opportunity cost from delayed truth |
| Staff time on reconciliation & report assembly | ETB **350K – 700K** | Management & clerk hours that do not add commercial value |
| **Indicative annual leakage (combined)** | **ETB 4.0M – 8.5M** | Before reputational / buyer-trust impact |

Even at the **low end (~ETB 4 million per year)**, three years of unchanged practice imply **ETB 12 million+** in cumulative operational leakage — many times the proposed system investment — before counting lost export opportunities from weak documentation and lot history.

### 3.3 Strategic risk: technology is no longer optional

Buyers, financiers, and regulators increasingly expect **traceability, consistent grading records, and digital shipment documentation**. Competitors who can prove lot origin, process history, and warehouse custody win preferred contracts and faster payment cycles.

If Leli Trading continues to run multi-warehouse coffee operations primarily on manual tools:

- Annual leakage compounds while margins tighten.  
- Export and local channels collide on the same stock.  
- Ownership spends more time reconciling than deciding.  
- Over a **3–5 year** horizon, the firm risks **progressive loss of competitiveness** — and, in the worst case, being forced to the margins of the market by operators who can already show digital control of every kilogram.

**The proposal is therefore not only an IT project; it is a business-continuity investment.**

## 4. Proposed solution

### 4.1 Solution overview

Csolve Technology will configure, deploy, and enable **Csolve** — an integrated Coffee Stock and Warehouse Management System — for Leli Trading, covering:

- End-to-end lot-based coffee tracking (cherry → parchment → green → roast/pack → sale/export)  
- Multi-location warehouse inventory with transfers, reservations, and adjustments  
- Local and export commercial channels with stock deductions tied to lots  
- Role-based access, approval workflows, and audit trails  
- Management dashboards and operational / financial reports  

The system is web-based, role-secured, and designed for daily use by storekeepers, processors, sales, export officers, finance, and executives.

### 4.2 How Csolve addresses the warehouse-tracking headache

| Ownership need | How Csolve responds |
|----------------|---------------------|
| Know where every lot is | Stock levels always by **location + lot** |
| Know what happened to it | Append-only **lot event timeline** (collect, process, transfer, adjust, sell, ship) |
| Control movement between stores | Formal **stock transfers** with status and accountability |
| Separate local vs export demand | **Channel-aware sales** and **export allocation / reservation** so reserved kg cannot be sold locally by mistake |
| See losses honestly | Structured adjustments (moisture, shrinkage, QC reject, damage) instead of silent write-offs |
| Decide with current numbers | Dashboard and reports refreshed from live operations |

### 4.3 Expected business outcomes

| Outcome | Indicator of success |
|---------|----------------------|
| Traceability | Lot history from intake to local sale or export ship |
| Inventory accuracy | Lower variance at stock counts; reserved vs available clearly shown |
| Operational efficiency | Fewer phone reconciliations; faster daily close |
| Accountability | Named users, permissions, and approval steps on sensitive stock actions |
| Decision quality | Consolidated stock, purchase, production, sales, export, and cash views |

## 5. System modules and features

The solution maps directly to Leli Trading’s required scope.

### 5.1 Coffee cherry management

- Receiving tickets with supplier / collector linkage  
- Weighing and quantity capture into lot-linked stock  
- Intake quality notes and acceptance flow  
- Automatic creation of the commercial purchase trail alongside physical intake  

### 5.2 Parchment coffee management

- Process runs that convert cherry / parchment with recorded input, output, loss, and reject  
- Drying and storage visibility through location and lot form  
- Quality attributes retained on the lot (grade, moisture, QC phase)  
- Stock movements between process and warehouse locations  

### 5.3 Green coffee management

- Hulling / cleaning / grading process stages with yield tracking  
- Warehouse inventory by lot, grade, and location  
- Batch (lot) codes and parent/child genealogy where splits occur  
- Inter-warehouse **stock transfers** with full audit  

### 5.4 Roasting, grinding, packaging, and local sales

- Local-market process workflows (roast, grind, pack) with material consumption  
- Finished-product inventory (including packaged SKUs)  
- Local-channel sales invoices with lot-linked deductions  
- Payment methods (cash, bank, credit, partial) and customer credit standing  

### 5.5 Export management

- Export contracts / orders with buyer and commercial terms  
- Batch allocation and **hard reservation** of kilograms  
- Document checklist and ECTA / quality attachments on export-store lots  
- Staging and shipment with stock OUT and optional linked export sale  
- Readiness visibility (contract, documents, ECTA, ship-ready)  

### 5.6 Warehouse and inventory management

- Multi-location stock ledger (warehouse, roastery, export staging, etc.)  
- Real-time on-hand, reserved, and available quantities  
- Stock adjustments with reason codes and lot events  
- Approval workflows for sensitive stock-impacting documents  
- Role- and location-scoped access for storekeepers and managers  

### 5.7 Management dashboard, reports, and people controls

- Consolidated operational dashboard (intake, process WIP, warehouse, local, export)  
- Sales, purchases, expenses, credits, and bank / cash trails  
- Profit & loss and analytical reports for management  
- Users / roles / permissions (People & HR directory for operational staff)  
- Notification of exceptions and pending approvals  

### 5.8 Cross-cutting platform capabilities

- Secure login with permission-gated navigation  
- Full audit trail on lot and document lifecycle  
- Dual currency awareness for export (e.g. USD) alongside ETB operations  
- Scalable web architecture suitable for multi-warehouse growth

## 6. Implementation methodology

Csolve Technology follows a structured delivery approach that minimizes disruption to ongoing coffee seasons.

| Phase | Focus | Client involvement |
|-------|-------|--------------------|
| **Discover** | Confirm warehouse map, process stages, roles, and opening balances approach | Ownership + operations leads |
| **Configure** | Locations, items/SKUs, process templates, roles, approval rules, numbering | Ops + IT contact |
| **Pilot** | One primary warehouse / process path live with real lots | Storekeeper + process supervisor |
| **Roll out** | Remaining locations, export desk, sales, finance views | Cross-functional users |
| **Stabilize** | Training reinforcement, data clean-up, go-live support | All named users |

**Principles**

1. Configure around Leli Trading’s real warehouses and coffee forms — not generic retail inventory.  
2. Protect the season: pilot on a controlled path before full cut-over.  
3. Prefer opening balances and lot migration plans agreed with ownership before go-live.  
4. Measure success by **warehouse reconciliation quality**, not by screens delivered alone.
